// Features

What makes Hex Compiler different

A story-driven idle experience where the interface itself is part of the narrative. The more magic you preserve, the more the world stabilizes.

01

Progressive UI stabilization

Start in a broken, glitched interface at Tier 0. As you advance through five design tiers, colors return, animations unlock, sound effects emerge, and procedural music restores the world.

02

Cast, craft, ascend

Cast spells to generate Arcane Bits. Craft preservation chambers to automate production. Prestige to earn Eldritch Keys and unlock permanent Boons and elemental specializations.

03

Experimentation and discovery

Run protocols in the Lab to discover hidden recipes. Combine elements in new ways to unlock powerful ingredients and advance your preservation strategy.

04

Meditation tower defense

Unlock a tower-defense meditation mini-game after your first ascension. Build focus, defend tranquility, and earn production bonuses that persist across sessions.

05

Achievements and daily rituals

Earn achievements for milestones and complete daily rituals for bonus rewards. The coven keeps you coming back with fresh objectives every day.

06

Offline PWA support

Install Hex Compiler to your home screen and play offline. Your progress auto-saves locally with an IndexedDB backup mirror for extra durability.

// Quickstart

How to play

Three simple steps to begin preserving magic.

01

Cast

Click the EXEC button to execute protocols and generate Arcane Bits and elemental essence.

02

Craft

Visit /MNT/WORKSTATIONS to craft preservation chambers that generate resources automatically.

03

Ascend

When your progress stalls, perform SYSTEM_ASCENSION to convert lifetime gains into permanent Eldritch Keys.
