How to Play Spellwright

🎯 Goal: Digitize & Preserve Magic

In a distant future where magic and technology converged, magic is now fading. Once it's gone, it won't return.

As a Code Witch, you're one of the last who can digitize magic—writing code that transforms fading spells into permanent data structures. Build digital preservation chambers. Program arcane algorithms. Compile magic into code. Every line you write saves what's being lost.

⚡ Getting Started

  1. Cast Spells - Click the "Cast" button to gather elemental essences before they fade (Fire, Water, Air, Crystal, Aether)
  2. Build Preservation Chambers - Use essences to craft workstations that automatically preserve magic and generate Spell Energy (SE)
  3. Earn SE - Spell Energy is the main currency for building advanced preservation chambers and techniques
  4. Unlock Inscriptions - Purchase permanent upgrades that make your preservation work more effective

📑 Tabs Explained

  • Workstations - Build preservation chambers to generate resources automatically
  • Inscriptions - Permanent upgrades that boost your production
  • Inventory - View all your collected materials
  • Experiment - Discover new recipes by combining ingredients
  • Rituals - Daily tasks for bonus rewards
  • Boons - Spend Eldritch Keys on permanent bonuses
  • Meditation - Tower defense mini-game to earn Focus
  • Stats - Track your achievements and milestones

⏫ Progression Path

  1. Cast spells → Gather essences
  2. Craft basic workstations (Candle, Cauldron)
  3. Earn AB from workstations
  4. Build advanced workstations
  5. Unlock Inscriptions for permanent boosts
  6. Experiment to discover new recipes
  7. Ascend (Prestige) to earn Eldritch Keys
  8. Spend Keys on powerful Boons

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • 1-8 - Switch between tabs
  • Ctrl + , - Open Settings
  • Esc - Close modals
  • Tab - Navigate between elements
  • Enter/Space - Activate buttons

💡 Tips

  • The game continues running when you're away - offline progress included!
  • Focus on one type of workstation at first to maximize efficiency
  • Inscriptions are expensive but give permanent benefits
  • Experiment often - some recipes are very powerful
  • Meditation is optional but provides useful Focus bonuses
  • Don't rush to Ascend - build up first for better returns

💾 Saving

Your game saves automatically every few seconds to your browser's local storage. You can also export/import saves from the Settings tab.
