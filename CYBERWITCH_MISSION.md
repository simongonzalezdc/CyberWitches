# Cyberwitch Mission & Currency Redesign

## 🎯 The Mission of the Cyberwitch

### Core Mission Statement

**"To digitize magic and build a self-sustaining cyber coven - an automated network of magical workstations that transcend the boundaries between technology and arcane power."**

### The Cyberwitch's Journey

1. **Early Game (Tier 0-1):** Cast spells manually to gather raw magical energy
2. **Mid Game (Tier 2-3):** Build workstations to automate magic production
3. **Late Game (Tier 4-5):** Create quantum and void-level infrastructure
4. **End Game (Prestige):** Ascend to higher planes, carrying forward accumulated power

### What Cyberwitches Are Building

- **A Digital Coven:** A network of automated magical workstations
- **Transcendent Power:** The ability to digitize and automate magic itself
- **Infinite Growth:** Self-sustaining systems that produce magic without manual intervention
- **Ascension:** The ability to transcend physical limitations through prestiges

## 💰 Currency Redesign: AB → Spell Energy (SE) ✅ COMPLETED

### Previous Problem

- **AB (Aether Bytes)** was confusing - sounded like it was related to the Aether element
- No clear connection to the Cyberwitch mission
- Doesn't explain what the currency represents

### Proposed New Names

#### Option 1: **"Spell Energy" (SE)** ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Why:** Directly represents energy gathered from casting spells
- **Function:** The power/energy needed to build and power magical workstations
- **Theme:** Energy from spells fuels the cyber coven
- **Abbreviation:** SE (clean, simple)

#### Option 2: **"Coven Power" (CP)**
- **Why:** Represents the power needed to build and maintain the coven
- **Function:** The collective power of the cyber coven
- **Theme:** Power that grows as the coven expands
- **Abbreviation:** CP (might be confused with other games)

#### Option 3: **"Digital Mana" (DM)**
- **Why:** Represents mana in digital/cyber form
- **Function:** The digitized magical energy that powers workstations
- **Theme:** Magic transformed into digital form
- **Abbreviation:** DM (might be confused with Dungeon Master)

#### Option 4: **"Spell Cores" (SC)**
- **Why:** Represents the core essence extracted from spells
- **Function:** The fundamental building blocks of magical automation
- **Theme:** Cores that power the cyber infrastructure
- **Abbreviation:** SC (clean, thematic)

#### Option 5: **"Arcane Power" (AP)**
- **Why:** Represents arcane power in general
- **Function:** The power needed to build arcane workstations
- **Theme:** General arcane power (less specific to cyber theme)
- **Abbreviation:** AP (might be confused with Action Points)

## 🎯 Recommended Choice: **"Spell Energy" (SE)**

### Why Spell Energy Works Best

1. **Clear Function:** Energy gathered from casting spells
2. **Mission Alignment:** Energy needed to build and power the cyber coven
3. **Intuitive:** Players understand "energy" as a resource
4. **No Confusion:** Completely separate from Aether element materials
5. **Thematic:** Fits the "cyber witch" theme - digitizing magical energy
6. **Scalable:** Works at all tiers (early game manual casting → late game automation)

### How Spell Energy Fits the Mission

- **Early Game:** Cast spells manually to gather Spell Energy
- **Mid Game:** Use Spell Energy to build workstations that automate production
- **Late Game:** Workstations produce Spell Energy automatically, creating a self-sustaining system
- **End Game:** Accumulate Spell Energy to transcend through prestiges

### Visual Identity

- **Color:** Gold/Yellow (energy/power theme)
- **Icon:** ⚡ (lightning bolt) or 🔋 (battery) or ✨ (sparkles)
- **Display:** "SE: 1,234" or "Spell Energy: 1,234"
