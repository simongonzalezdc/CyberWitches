## Getting Started

### First Launch

When you first launch the game, you'll see a minimal interface (Tier 0). This is intentional! The game uses a **Design Tier System** that progressively unlocks features as you play.

### Tutorial

If you're a new player, the tutorial will automatically start. Follow the on-screen instructions to learn the basics:
1. Cast your first spell
2. Build your first workstation
3. Purchase your first upgrade

You can skip the tutorial at any time, or restart it later from the Settings tab.

## Game Basics

### Currency

**AB (Arcane Bits)** - Your primary currency
- Earned by casting spells
- Produced automatically by workstations
- Used to craft workstations and purchase upgrades

**Eldritch Keys (EK)** - Prestige currency
- Earned by ascending (prestiging)
- Used to purchase permanent bonuses (Boons)
- Unlocks new features (Meditation, Boons tabs)

### Casting Spells

Click the **Cast** button (or press **Space**) to cast a spell. Each cast:
- Earns AB and ingredients
- Has a chance for bonus multipliers
- Progresses quests and achievements

### Workstations

Workstations automatically produce ingredients and AB over time. To craft a workstation:
1. Go to the **Workstations** tab
2. Find a workstation you can afford
3. Click **"Craft x1"** (or craft multiple at once)

**Tip:** Workstations are the key to idle progression. Build them early and often!

### Upgrades (Inscriptions)

Inscriptions are permanent upgrades that boost your production. To purchase:
1. Go to the **Inscriptions** tab
2. Find an upgrade you can afford
3. Click **"Purchase"**

**Tip:** Focus on upgrades that multiply your production first!

## Core Systems

### Design Tier System

The game progressively unlocks visual and audio features:

- **Tier 0 (Minimal):** Monochrome, no animations, no sound
- **Tier 1 (Basic Colors):** Unlocks at first achievement OR 100 AB
- **Tier 2 (Sound Effects):** Unlocks at first ascension OR 1,000 AB
- **Tier 3 (Full Graphics):** Unlocks at second ascension OR 10,000 AB
- **Tier 4 (Music):** Unlocks at third ascension OR 100,000 AB

**Tip:** You can manually select your tier in Settings after your first ascension!

### Prestige System (Ascension)

When you're ready to reset your progress for permanent bonuses:
1. Go to the **Stats** tab
2. Click **"Ascend"** (or press **P**)
3. Confirm your ascension

**What you keep:**
- Prestige points (Eldritch Keys)
- Prestige bonuses (Boons)
- Discovered recipes
- Achievements
- Design tier unlocks

**What you lose:**
- All AB and ingredients
- All workstations
- All upgrades (except prestige bonuses)

**Tip:** Ascend when you feel progression has slowed down. The permanent bonuses make future runs faster!

### Daily Rituals

Complete daily tasks to earn rewards:
1. Go to the **Rituals** tab
2. Complete tasks (cast spells, craft workstations, etc.)
3. Claim rewards (AB, ingredients, Eldritch Key fragments)

**Tip:** Check daily rituals every day for free rewards!

### Meditation Mini-Game

Unlocked after your first ascension. A tower defense mini-game where you:
- Defend against waves of enemies
- Place towers to defeat enemies
- Earn Focus currency
- Unlock meditation upgrades
- Gain production bonuses

**Tip:** Meditation bonuses apply to your main game production!

### Experimentation

Discover new recipes by experimenting:
1. Go to the **Experiment** tab
2. Click **"Try Experiment"**
3. Combine ingredients to discover new recipes

**Tip:** Experimentation costs AB, but discovered recipes are permanent!

## Progression

### Early Game (0 - 1,000 AB)

**Focus:**
- Cast spells manually
- Build your first workstations
- Purchase basic upgrades
- Complete the tutorial

**Goals:**
- Reach 100 AB (unlocks Tier 1)
- Build 5+ workstations
- Purchase 3+ upgrades

### Mid Game (1,000 - 100,000 AB)

**Focus:**
- Automate production with workstations
- Purchase production multipliers
- Complete daily rituals
- Experiment with recipes

**Goals:**
- Reach 1,000 AB (unlocks Tier 2)
- Build 20+ workstations
- Purchase 10+ upgrades
- Discover 5+ recipes

### Late Game (100,000+ AB)

**Focus:**
- Optimize production
- Prepare for first ascension
- Complete achievements
- Unlock all design tiers

**Goals:**
- Reach 10,000 AB (unlocks Tier 3)
- Build 50+ workstations
- Purchase all affordable upgrades
- Complete 10+ achievements

### Post-Ascension

**Focus:**
- Purchase Boons with Eldritch Keys
- Unlock Meditation mini-game
- Optimize for faster progression
- Complete more ascensions

**Goals:**
- Purchase 5+ Boons
- Complete Meditation waves
- Reach Tier 4 (Music)
- Complete 20+ achievements

## Tips & Strategies

### General Tips

1. **Idle is Key:** Let workstations produce while you're away
2. **Balance Active and Idle:** Cast spells actively, but let workstations do the heavy lifting
3. **Focus on Multipliers:** Production multipliers compound over time
4. **Complete Daily Rituals:** Free rewards every day
5. **Experiment Regularly:** Discovered recipes are permanent

### Workstation Strategy

1. **Build Early:** Workstations are your main source of production
2. **Focus on Efficiency:** Some workstations are more efficient than others
3. **Balance Production:** Don't just focus on AB - ingredients are important too
4. **Upgrade Before Crafting:** Production multipliers apply to all workstations

### Upgrade Strategy

1. **Global Multipliers First:** These affect all production
2. **Production Multipliers:** These compound over time
3. **Cost Reductions:** These help you craft more workstations
4. **Save for Expensive Upgrades:** Some upgrades are game-changers

### Prestige Strategy

1. **Ascend When Stuck:** If progression has slowed, it's time to ascend
2. **Save Eldritch Keys:** Some Boons are more valuable than others
3. **Focus on Production Boons:** These make future runs faster
4. **Multiple Ascensions:** Each ascension makes the next one easier

### Meditation Strategy

1. **Defend Early Waves:** Focus on survival first
2. **Upgrade Towers:** Upgraded towers are more effective
3. **Balance Resources:** Don't spend all Focus on one thing
4. **Production Bonuses:** Meditation bonuses apply to main game

## Troubleshooting

### Game Won't Load

1. **Clear Browser Cache:** Old cached files may cause issues
2. **Check Browser Console:** Press F12 to see error messages
3. **Try Different Browser:** Some browsers may have compatibility issues
4. **Disable Extensions:** Browser extensions may interfere

### Save Data Issues

1. **Check localStorage:** Make sure your browser allows localStorage
2. **Export Save:** Use Settings to export your save data
3. **Import Save:** Use Settings to import your save data
4. **Backup Regularly:** Export your save data regularly

### Performance Issues

1. **Lower Design Tier:** Use a lower tier in Settings
2. **Enable Low Power Mode:** Reduces animations and effects
3. **Reduce Animation Quality:** Lower quality uses less battery
4. **Close Other Tabs:** Free up browser resources

### Audio Issues

1. **Check Tier:** Audio requires Tier 2+ (sound effects) or Tier 4+ (music)
2. **Check Volume:** Use Settings to adjust volume sliders
3. **Browser Permissions:** Some browsers require user interaction to play audio
4. **Check Mute:** Make sure your device isn't muted

## FAQ

### Q: How do I save my progress?

**A:** The game auto-saves every 30 seconds. You can also manually export your save data from Settings.

### Q: Can I play offline?

**A:** Yes! The game works offline. Your progress is saved locally in your browser.

### Q: Will I lose my progress if I clear my browser cache?

**A:** Yes, if you clear localStorage. Always export your save data before clearing cache!

### Q: How do I reset my progress?

**A:** Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Reset All Progress. **Warning:** This cannot be undone!

### Q: What happens when I ascend?

**A:** You reset your AB, ingredients, workstations, and upgrades, but keep Eldritch Keys, Boons, recipes, achievements, and design tier unlocks.

### Q: How do I unlock Meditation?

**A:** Meditation unlocks after your first ascension.

### Q: How do I unlock Boons?

**A:** Boons unlock after your first ascension. Use Eldritch Keys to purchase them.

### Q: What are Eldritch Key fragments?

**A:** Fragments are earned from daily rituals. 10 fragments = 1 Eldritch Key.

### Q: How do I change my design tier?

**A:** Go to Settings → Design Tier. You can only select tiers you've unlocked.

### Q: Can I play on mobile?

**A:** Yes! The game is fully responsive and works on mobile devices.

### Q: How do I report bugs?

**A:** Check the browser console (F12) for error messages. Report bugs with console logs if possible.

### Q: Is there a way to speed up the game?

**A:** The game runs at a fixed speed. Focus on production multipliers to progress faster!

### Q: What's the best strategy?

**A:** There's no single "best" strategy. Experiment and find what works for you! Generally, focus on workstations and production multipliers.
