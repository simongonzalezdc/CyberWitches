# Cyber Witches - User Guide

## Getting Started

### First Steps

1. **Cast Your First Spell**: Click the "Cast" button to earn your first ingredients and AB (Alchemical Bits)
2. **Build Workstations**: Visit the Workstations tab to craft buildings that automatically produce ingredients
3. **Purchase Upgrades**: Go to the Inscriptions tab to buy permanent upgrades
4. **Experiment**: Try the Experiment tab to discover new recipes

## Game Mechanics

### Currency

- **AB (Alchemical Bits)**: Main currency, earned by casting and workstations
- **Elements**: Fire, Water, Air, Crystal, Aether, and Focus - used for crafting

### Workstations

Workstations automatically produce ingredients over time. Each workstation:
- Has a base production rate
- Costs ingredients to craft
- Costs increase with each craft (growth factor)
- Can be upgraded with Inscriptions

### Inscriptions (Upgrades)

Permanent upgrades that enhance your production:
- **Multiplier Upgrades**: Increase production rates
- **Additive Upgrades**: Add flat bonuses
- **Global Upgrades**: Affect all production
- **Producer-Specific**: Only affect certain workstations

### Prestige System (Ascension)

After earning enough AB, you can Ascend:
- Resets most progress
- Grants Eldritch Keys (EK)
- Unlocks permanent bonuses
- Unlocks new features (Meditation, Boons)

### Design Tiers

The game progressively unlocks visual and audio features:
- **Tier 0**: Minimal (text only)
- **Tier 1**: Basic colors
- **Tier 2**: Sound effects
- **Tier 3**: Full graphics and animations
- **Tier 4**: Music and advanced features

Tiers unlock as you progress and achieve milestones.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Space**: Cast spell
- **1-9**: Switch tabs (1=Workstations, 2=Inscriptions, etc.)
- **S**: Save game
- **A**: Open Ascension modal (if available)
- **Ctrl+,**: Open Settings
- **Escape**: Close modals

## Tips & Strategies

1. **Early Game**: Focus on casting and building your first workstations
2. **Mid Game**: Balance workstation production with upgrades
3. **Late Game**: Plan for ascension and prestige bonuses
4. **Optimization**: Build workstations that produce ingredients you need most
5. **Experimentation**: Try the Experiment tab to discover powerful recipes

## Troubleshooting

### Game Not Saving
- Check browser localStorage permissions
- Ensure you have storage space available
- Try refreshing the page

### Performance Issues
- Enable Low Power Mode in Settings
- Reduce Animation Quality
- Enable Mobile Performance Mode (on mobile)

### Audio Not Working
- Check browser audio permissions
- Ensure Design Tier is 2+ for sounds, 4+ for music
- Check volume sliders in Settings
